Limits (read before quoting any number)

  • Synthetic data, generated with known ground truth. Message shapes resemble four common monitoring tools but are not vendor formats.
  • The event reduction depends strongly on the data. A small asset model makes storms collapse more than a real CMDB would.
  • Labels (noise / critical) are defined by the generator. On real data labelling is human work and the most sensitive part of the measurement.
  • The repeat filter can merge a new, separate incident into an earlier identical alert. The kit counts these cases; they are not silent losses, but they distort incident counts.
  • The error-and-above view relies on normalised severities; the built-in tool mappings are typical scales, not vendor specifications, and must be checked against the real values (evalkit import lists unmapped ones).
